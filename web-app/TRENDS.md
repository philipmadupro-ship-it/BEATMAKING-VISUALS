# TRENDS.md — beat-preview research for U2C Studio (researched 2026-11-12)

Caveat: almost every source is a vendor/SEO blog. No platform publishes organic safe-zone pixels, no source gives beat-specific retention data, and nothing was found on Kollide/noteframe. Treat numbers as defaults to test, not rules. Items marked (UNVERIFIED) are single-source or vendor claims.

## ACTIONABLE FOR THE APP
1. Default hook clip = 15s, with 30s and 60s as alternates (7s "loop" preset too). Hook export starts exactly on a downbeat, frame 0 already shows the full visual and the beat sounding; no fade-in, no silent lead-in.
2. Add a "loop-safe" export mode: end on a bar boundary, pick an end point whose last beat resolves into the first frame (crossfade audio 1-2 frames, ending visual state == frame 0 state) so replays feel endless.
3. Safe-area overlay for 9:16 (1080x1920). Hard default text box: x 162-918, y 288-1248 (most conservative, 756x960). Relaxed box: x 108-972, y 288-1440. Margins: top 288, bottom 480-672 (use 672 for CTA/price text), sides 108-162. Label as approximate; offer "check in app" reminder.
4. Text-overlay presets (mono label style, inside safe box): top line `[FREE] ARTIST x ARTIST TYPE BEAT`; second line `"BEAT NAME"`; chip row `142 BPM · F# MIN`; CTA line `LEASE $29 — LINK IN BIO` (bottom edge of safe box, never in bottom 672 px except the optional handle); `PROD. @uche2crazy`. Each element toggleable, drag-able.
5. Title builder: default `[FREE] {Artist} x {Artist} Type Beat - "{Name}" | {BPM} BPM`, producer profile remembers format; also emits copy-ready caption, description (store link in first 2 lines), 5-10 tags (artist names, genre, BPM, mood). Keep key phrase in first 60-70 chars.
6. YouTube 16:9 export: 1920x1080 MP4/AAC, full-length beat (2-4 min typical), plus a thumbnail generator (1280x720 JPG/PNG): big beat name + artist cue, high contrast, keep bottom-right ~15% clear for the duration badge. Offer 2-3 thumbnail variants for YouTube A/B testing.
7. Export defaults: H.264 High, 1080x1920 (1920x1080 for YT), 30 fps (60 optional), 10-12 Mbps VBR, AAC 48 kHz 256-320 kbps, MP4, faststart. Cap preset: Reels/Shorts <=3 min, TikTok keep <=60s for hook clips.
8. Audio: loudness-normalize export to -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP ceiling (user toggle: -14 / -12 "louder" / off). Show measured LUFS/true-peak in Export tab. Never add a watermark (IG suppresses watermarked content).
9. Voice-tag option: optional uploaded/recorded tag clip placed at an adjustable offset (default 1.5s in? or at 0 beat) and repeated every ~25s (SoundPage uses ~25s); 3-7s long, mixed just under the lead melody. Always offer "clean export" next to "tagged" and remember which is the public preview.
10. Reactivity must be hard-visible within the first 1-2s: first bar should show the strongest kick-synced event (chain assemble + glitch hit on beat 1), since hook rate (first ~3s) is the key metric. Auto-hook picker should score "drop/energy" and prefer sections whose beat 1 is a transient.
11. Templates to add or tune (matched to 2026 trends): EXE (Y2K OS window, already), HANDSET (dither/halftone), LAB (wireframe), plus "CHROME" (3D chrome wordmark, iridescent gradients using only palette hues), "KINETIC" (big kinetic type of BPM/key/name locked to beats), "SCOPE" (spectrum/waveform scanner). Kinetic typography + waveform are table stakes; glitch/pixelation is now a deliberate feature.
12. "DAW-screen" template: fake-FL-style playlist/piano-roll panel animated from the analysed track (notes appear on kicks/snares) with a "cooking" caption variant; DAW screen recordings are the dominant producer-audience format and plain waveform recordings are called stale/outdated.
13. Motion always on: slow zoom, grain, dust/particles on every template (continuous motion beats static art on watch time, UNVERIFIED vendor claim); include cover-art blur background option.
14. Caption/hashtag helper per platform: short caption (1 line + CTA), 3-5 tags e.g. #typebeat #{genre}typebeat #beatmaker #producer #{artist}typebeat; export `.txt` alongside video. Batch export a 3-up set (7s loop, 15s, 30s) per template to support 3-7 posts/week cadence.
15. Posting-time guidance in UI = "test your own": suggest 4-7pm weekday and 7-10pm evening windows, 3-7 posts/week, tracked as a simple local note (evidence is weak and contradictory).

## Q1 Formats and reach
- Producer-audience formats: DAW screen recording with text/voiceover (claimed +15-25% follower growth from aspiring producers, UNVERIFIED), build-from-scratch, challenge/remake of viral sound. https://musictech.com/features/opinion-analysis/new-soundcloud-era-youtube-daw-screen-captures/ , https://subscribr.ai/p/beatmaking-youtube-video-ideas , https://fluxnote.io/guides/tiktok-beatmaking-strategy
- Artist/buyer-audience formats: visualizer or mood visual cut to the hook/drop, 15-60s. https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-to-make-type-beat-videos , https://freebeat.ai/articles/instagram-reel-ideas-promote-your-beat
- Plain waveform-over-beat recording called "ancient"; viewers rarely stop. https://splice.com/blog/?p=39805
- Mood-matched visuals (e.g. night street) argued to beat DAW footage for chill beats (one blog). https://strokecast.com/?p=3420
- Instagram reportedly suppresses watermarked content (vendor claim). Same freebeat/strokecast sources.
- Not found: any evidence on "reaction", lyric-style, POV-caption, or "type beat thumbnail" formats on shorts. No comparative data between formats.
- Takeaway: ship both visualizer templates (artist reach) and a DAW-screen style (producer reach); test.

## Q2 Preview specifics
- Length: TikTok music clips 15-30s with hook in first 3s (https://dynamoi.com/learn/faqs/what-is-the-best-length-for-tiktok-music-clips); 15-60s for beat clips (fluxnote); 7-15s starter Reels (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts); one 2025 analysis found 60-90s Reels got highest average views (same source). Under-7s micro clips trending 2026 (https://www.capcut.com/create/micro-storytelling-reels-under-7-seconds).
- Hook: viewers decide in under 1.5-3s; cut on beat 1, no fade, no silence (https://nestscale.com/?p=47200 and results above). Loop structure: seconds 1-3 hook, final 2 seconds resolve to loop point (freebeat/beatsync-style guides; single-source).
- Text: BPM/key, type-beat name, price/link in bio, producer tag are all standard; no source gave tested layouts, so presets above are our design.
- Cadence/time: 3-7 posts/week on each platform; best-time claims contradict (TikTok 4-7pm weekdays or 7-10pm; Reels 9-11am/11-1pm vs. overnight; Shorts 3-7pm Fri/Sat). Test your own. https://clipspeed-site.pages.dev/blog/best-posting-times-social-media.html , https://autoclip.dev/blog/best-posting-times-for-clips

## Q5 Audio
- -14 LUFS integrated / -1 dBTP is the consensus target (YouTube confirmed behaviour: only turns loud audio down); TikTok/IG have no published spec, -14 to -16 safe; one vendor claims -10 to -12 preferred (UNVERIFIED). https://www.forasoft.com/learn/audio-for-video/articles-audio/lufs-targets-per-platform-2026 , https://openclip.app/learn/audio-normalization , https://opus.pro/blog/best-loudness-normalizers
- Voice tags: 3-7s typical (some 1-3s); public previews tagged, buyers get untagged; repeat ~every 25s (SoundPage default), mix just under lead; many producers use one at start. https://violetrecording.com/how-to-tag-your-beats/ , https://soundpage.notion.site/Producer-tags-Bulk-Uploads-Only-37873072fc8581a28547c3c01bf67c5f , https://orphiq.com/resources/producer-tags-sonic-branding
- Fade vs hard loop: no source tested; guidance is "no fade-in, cut on beat 1". Our recommendation: hard cut in, bar-aligned loop point, short (<=1 frame-ish) crossfade at seam.

## Q6 Funnel
- Store link always in bio (one URL per platform; a link hub is common); beat store first; lead magnet (email -> free beats) pairs with it. https://hiphopmakers.com/how-to-sell-beats-on-instagram , https://www.unilink.us/blog/best-link-in-bio-for-music-producers
- Free vs lease: free downloads are restricted-use (usually non-profit/tagged, label clearly); non-exclusive lease lets same beat sell to many; exclusive removes it from sale. https://www.sendowl.com/blog/tips-and-advice/how-to-sell-beats-online , https://payhip.com/Claudestyleebeats/blog/beat-leasing-vs-royalty-free-licensing-what-s-the-difference/beat-leasing-vs-royalty-free-licensing
